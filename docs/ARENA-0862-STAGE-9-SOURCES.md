# Cambridge Lower Secondary Mathematics 0862 Stage 9

The course is for learners aged 13–14. Its first nine chapters follow the Stage 9 Scheme of Work. The final chapter provides mixed practice with and without a calculator. The order and objective codes come from the supplied Cambridge framework, rather than from the sequence used in the supplementary books or websites. [Cambridge Lower Secondary Mathematics](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-lower-secondary/curriculum/mathematics/)

## Teaching sources

- The supplied *0862 Mathematics Curriculum Framework*, printed pages 27–32, defines the 55 Stage 9 mathematics objectives. The Stage 9 Scheme of Work puts them into units 9.1–9.9. The long-term planning template and Teacher Guide informed the use of planned revisiting, short diagnostic questions, worked examples, student explanation and reflection.
- The supplied Pimentel, Pimentel and Wall *Cambridge Checkpoint Lower Secondary Mathematics Student's Book 9*, third edition, was used to cross-check the order of ideas within topics. The notes align its units 1, 9, 11 and 26 to number, units 5, 15, 18 and 20 to algebra, units 2, 4, 6 and 8 to shapes and measures, units 14, 22 and 29 to calculation, units 10, 16 and 19 to probability, units 17 and 28 to angles, units 21, 24, 25, 27 and 30 to graphs, units 13 and 23 to transformations, and units 3, 7 and 12 to statistics. [Cambridge resource list](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-lower-secondary/resource-list/cambridge-lower-secondary-mathematics-0862/Index.aspx)
- Oak's Year 9 sequence informed approaches to index laws, standard form, equivalent representations, Pythagoras and probability. The course builds visible steps from a concrete display or situation to a written rule and an independent check. [Oak Year 9 maths units](https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/programmes/maths-secondary/units?keystages=ks3&years=9) · [Oak Pythagoras unit](https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/programmes/maths-secondary-ks3/units/geometrical-properties-similarity-and-pythagoras-theorem/lessons)
- Corbettmaths provides further examples of how to teach standard form, sharing in a ratio, bearings, circle measures, polygons, transformations, drawing graphs and comparing data. The Maths4U worked explanations and visuals use their own figures and data. [Corbettmaths topics](https://corbettmaths.com/contents/)

## Assessment sources

The supplied 2025 Stage 9 Progression Test question papers and mark schemes under D:/math 7-8 informed the mix of quick numerical problems, diagrams, written explanations and multi-step reasoning. Paper 1 is non-calculator and Paper 2 allows a calculator. Each supplied paper is 50 marks in one hour. The Stage 7 and Stage 8 files labelled 3141 and 3142 are earlier-stage Progression Tests. Stage 9 is the point where Cambridge Checkpoint is designed to be taken. [Cambridge assessment overview](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-lower-secondary/assessment/) · [Cambridge Checkpoint age and stage](https://help.cambridgeinternational.org/hc/en-gb/articles/360000055018-Which-age-group-of-learners-is-the-Cambridge-Lower-Secondary-Checkpoint-for)

The October 2024 Checkpoint 0862 questions informed the mix of prompts and calculator expectations. The linked illustration of format is an archival copy of Paper 2. The on-site mixed practice uses shorter Maths4U sets. The test papers and mark schemes themselves stay in their source locations. [Archived Checkpoint 0862/02, October 2024](https://doyenpublishers.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/2024-OCT-LOWER-SEC-CHECKPOINT-MATH-PAPER-2.pdf) · [Cambridge guidance on finding past papers](https://help.cambridgeinternational.org/hc/en-gb/articles/23411548702482-Where-can-I-find-past-papers-for-Cambridge-Lower-Secondary-Checkpoint)

Each question family was authored around an objective and reasoning pattern. The wording, contexts, numbers, artwork and marking criteria are Maths4U's. Construction, freehand graph drawing and justification have worked solutions and marking guidance. Short checks for those lessons assess calculable steps, coordinates, angles or choices.

## Stage 9 coverage

| Lesson key | Framework codes | Evidence in lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| indices | 9Ni.02 | Multiply and divide powers of the same base. Explain zero and negative powers. |
| standard-form | 9Ni.03, 9Np.01 | Use positive and negative powers of ten. Read a coefficient and exponent from a measurement. |
| number-types | 9Ni.01, 9Ni.04 | Classify rational and irrational values and estimate square and cube roots between integers. |
| expressions | 9Ae.01, 9Ae.02, 9Ae.03 | Use order, double brackets, index laws and algebraic fractions. Translate verbal rules involving powers into symbols. |
| formulae | 9Ae.04 | Change the subject of linear, square and cube formulae by inverse operations. |
| equations-limits | 9Np.02, 9Ae.05, 9Ae.07 | Work with both rounding limits. Model an equation or inequality and deal with an unknown denominator. |
| linear-systems | 9Ae.06 | Use elimination, graphical intersection and a count-and-value model. |
| circles-compound | 9Gg.01, 9Gg.03 | Distinguish circle area and circumference. Join and remove rectangle, semicircle and triangle areas. |
| units-volume | 9Gg.02, 9Gg.04 | Convert small and large units of length, mass and capacity. Build volumes from triangular and circular cross-sections. |
| surface-symmetry | 9Gg.05, 9Gg.06 | Calculate faces of cuboids, cylinders, square pyramids and triangular prisms. Locate planes of symmetry. |
| pythagoras | 9Gg.10 | Find the direct distance, recover a leg and test for a right triangle. |
| fraction-sums | 9Nf.01, 9Nf.02 | Predict recurring or terminating decimals. Work with mixed, improper and simple fractions in sums and differences. |
| products-and-decimals | 9Nf.03, 9Nf.06 | Cancel factors and multiply by a reciprocal. Estimate and check decimal multiplication and division. |
| mixed-calculations | 9Nf.04, 9Nf.06 | Combine decimals, fractions, brackets and exponents. Multiply decimals by whole-number counts. |
| compound-percentages | 9Nf.05 | Calculate repeated rises, repeated falls and different successive changes. |
| ratio-models | 9Nf.07, 9Nf.08 | Share in a ratio, calculate a unit rate and hold a product fixed in inverse proportion. |
| outcomes | 9Sp.01, 9Sp.02, 9Sp.03 | Add disjoint events, use complements and compare with/without replacement. Use products and sums across branches. |
| expected-frequency | 9Sp.04 | Calculate and compare expected and observed frequencies. Design a simulation with different run sizes. |
| polygons-angles | 9Gg.07, 9Gg.08, 9Gg.09 | Explain interior and exterior angle sums and combine parallel-line rules with angle sums. |
| bearings-construction | 9Gg.11, 9Gp.01 | Read and reverse bearings, apply a scale, construct 30°, 45°, 60° and regular polygons. |
| sequencing | 9As.01, 9As.02 | Use term gaps, fractions of the position and square and cube rules, including shifts. |
| inputs-and-rules | 9As.03, 9As.04 | Use a function forwards and backwards, and translate a fixed starting value and rate to an equation. |
| draw-and-read-lines | 9As.05, 9As.06 | Tabulate implicit lines and shifted quadratics. Use signed and fractional gradients to find an equation. |
| compound-rates | 9As.07 | Read and compare gradients as speed and density. |
| rigid-moves | 9Gp.02, 9Gp.03, 9Gp.04, 9Gp.05 | Locate a point along a segment. Reflect, translate and rotate. Describe a transformation and justify congruence. |
| scale-and-area | 9Gp.06, 9Gp.07 | Enlarge around a centre, recover the factor and compare effects on perimeter and area. |
| data-collection | 9Ss.01, 9Ss.02 | Select data, trial a process and improve sampling after spotting bias. |
| categorical-displays | 9Ss.03 | Use Venn and Carroll regions, count tables, dual and compound bars, and pie sectors. |
| chart-choices | 9Ss.03, 9Ss.05 | Use time series, frequency polygons, scatter, and single and shared stem-and-leaf displays. |
| compare-and-question | 9Ss.04, 9Ss.05 | Compare means, medians, modes and ranges. Estimate grouped means and challenge misleading infographics and claims. |
| checkpoint-no-calculator | Mixed review | Use ratio and fraction arithmetic, Pythagoras and exact summary statistics. |
| checkpoint-calculator | Mixed review | Use repeated percentage multipliers, circle area and compound units. |

Thinking and working mathematically are practised across the lessons. Learners explain why a relation holds, compare strategies or cases, and improve a drawing, survey or conclusion in response to evidence.
