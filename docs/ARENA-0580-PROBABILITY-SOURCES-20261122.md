# Cambridge IGCSE 0580 · Probability

The first five lessons cover C8.1–C8.3 and the corresponding shared Extended skills. The final four cover Extended notation, three-set diagrams, draws without replacement and conditional probability. Core trees use replacement. The final lessons teach conditional probabilities by restricting possible outcomes in Venn diagrams, tables and trees. Cambridge makes conditional probability notation and formulae optional.
