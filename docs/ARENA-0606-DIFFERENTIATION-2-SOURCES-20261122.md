# 0606 Calculus: Differentiation 2

## Sources and teaching sequence

- The official Cambridge 0606 programme, objectives 14.3–14.9, requires the natural exponential, natural logarithm and three trigonometric derivatives in radians together with chain, product and quotient cases. The usual gradient, stationary point and second-derivative arguments remain applicable. [Cambridge 2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662470-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf)
- Sue Pemberton's third-edition coursebook in `D:/new_books/addMaths10.pdf`, §§14.1–14.4, provides the sequence of exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric functions followed by mixed derivatives. Each lesson in Arena uses its own explanations, models and values.
- Save My Exams separates the standard derivative from the additional inner derivative and keeps angles in radians. Arena uses this progression and then adds independent cases where the learner must select a method. [Differentiating Special Functions](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/further-maths/cie/additional-maths/25/revision-notes/calculus/differentiation/differentiating-special-functions/), [Applications of Differentiation](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/further-maths/cie/additional-maths/25/revision-notes/calculus/differentiation/applications-of-differentiation/)
- TLMaths presents the basic exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric profiles separately before blending operations. Arena follows that teaching progression and stays within the Cambridge 0606 objectives. [Differentiation and Further Differentiation](https://www.tlmaths.com/home/a-level-maths/2nd-year-only/g-differentiation/g4-further-differentiation)
