# Cambridge 0606 permutations and combinations

The chapter follows syllabus objectives 11.1–11.3. It teaches the product rule, factorials including zero factorial, restrictions in straight-line arrangements, permutations, combinations and algebraic reverse-count questions. All counted objects are distinct and are used at most once. Ordered and unordered selections have separate problems. [Cambridge syllabus 2025–2027, pp. 9 and 17](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662470-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf)
