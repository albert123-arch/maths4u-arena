# 0580 source audit for Fractions, decimals and percentages

## Scope and sequencing

The Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 [2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf) places terminology, equivalent forms and conversion in C1.4 and E1.4. Recurring notation and conversions involving recurring decimals belong to the Extended lesson. The [2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf) confirms the Core boundary for the next cycle. Ordering by magnitude, four operations, percentages of amounts and percentage change have their own syllabus topics.

The local **Cambridge IGCSE 0580/0980 Scheme of Work for examination from 2025**, PDF p. 14, introduces fraction and percentage terms, connects place value to a power-of-ten denominator and aligns recurring tails. The first three lessons follow this order. The Extended lesson relates repeated remainders to exact recurring notation before and after the algebraic conversion.
