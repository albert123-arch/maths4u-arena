# 0606 Logarithmic and exponential functions

The chapter covers Cambridge 0606 sections 6.1–6.3. It develops exponent laws, logarithms, changes of base, solving equations, the functions \(e^x\) and \(\ln x\), inverse functions, graph transformations, and exponential models. Numerical values requested to a stated precision are assessed at that precision. The exact forms are given in the worked solutions. [Cambridge 0606 syllabus for 2025–2027, pp. 14–15](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662470-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf)

The sequence follows Sue Pemberton, *Cambridge IGCSE and O Level Additional Mathematics Coursebook*, third edition, chapter 5, sections 5.1–5.11 in the supplied PDF. The worked explanations and transformations are new. The first lesson links an exponent statement back to its logarithmic statement. Later lessons show where the original domain must be checked before logarithms are combined, and where to reject a value after an exponential substitution. The extra practice gives separate opportunities to isolate an exponential, compare bases and select a valid root.
