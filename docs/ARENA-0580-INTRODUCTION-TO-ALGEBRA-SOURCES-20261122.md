# 0580 Introduction to algebra

C2.1 and E2.1 in the [Cambridge 2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf) ask learners to recognise letters as generalised numbers and substitute in expressions and formulas. The [2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf) retains those objectives. The supplied 0580/0980 Scheme of Work PDF, p. 10, and its DOCX use familiar quantities to introduce letters before numerical substitution.
