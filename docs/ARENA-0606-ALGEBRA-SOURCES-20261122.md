# 0606 algebra foundations sources

The four chapters follow the supplied Pemberton third-edition coursebook, sections 1.1–1.7 for Functions, 2.1–2.6 for Quadratic functions and simultaneous equations, 3.1–3.5 for Factors of polynomials and 4.1–4.5 for Equations, inequalities and graphs. Cambridge's first-derivative applications are taught later. The trigonometric graphs in the modulus objective are taught in the Trigonometry chapter. [Cambridge 0606 syllabus for 2025–2027](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662470-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf)

The 26 lessons contain 156 worked examples and 312 inline or extra checks. The written bank has 341 main exam-style questions, 1,023 fresh written variants, and 130 short questions with 130 fresh variants. The four chapter allocations match the past-paper catalogue counts. Independent proofs and computations in the preparation scripts check intervals, graph roots, polynomial divisions, paired solutions and restricted ranges.
