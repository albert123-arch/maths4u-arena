# 0606 Straight-line graphs

The seven lessons follow chapter 6 of the supplied Sue Pemberton *Additional Mathematics Coursebook*, sections 6.1–6.7. They move from distance, midpoint and gradient through parallel and perpendicular lines, equations, intersections and areas, then shift to transformed axes and data. The transformations include \(y=Ax^n\), \(y=Ab^x\), \(y^2=Ax^3+B\), \(e^{2y}=Ax^2+B\) and \(y^3=A\ln x+B\). Branch choices and the difference between original and transformed coordinates are made explicit. [Cambridge 0606 syllabus for 2025–2027, section 7](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662470-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf)

The line lessons use a rise–run triangle, an independently drawn perpendicular bisector, intersecting lines and an intercept triangle. Examples put each chosen point back into its equation. Lessons on transformed graphs use labelled HTML tables and reverse the transformation in the same order as the written working. The example coordinates, figures and explanations were composed for Arena.
