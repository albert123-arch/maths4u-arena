# 0580 Sketching curves

The [Cambridge 2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf) gives C2.11 line and quadratic sketches with symmetry and roots. E2.11 adds cubic, reciprocal and exponential forms, asymptotes and completing the square for quadratic turning points. The [2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf) was checked for the corresponding objectives. The supplied Scheme of Work, pp. 36–37 and the matching DOCX, contrasts a sketch that shows shape and key features with an accurately plotted graph.
