# Cambridge IGCSE 0580 · Graphs of functions

C2.10 covers tables, accurate plots and graphical interpretation of straight lines, quadratics with leading coefficient 1 or −1, and reciprocals. It includes graphical roots and simultaneous readings. E2.10 includes rational multiples of the powers −2, −1, −½, 0, ½, 1, 2 and 3, with sums of at most three terms. It also includes shifted exponential rules with integer bases and graphing growth and decay. The seven lessons develop those skills. General qualitative sketches and asymptote exercises belong to C/E2.11.
