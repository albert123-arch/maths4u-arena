# Cambridge Lower Secondary Mathematics 0862 Stage 7

## Curriculum and teaching sources

The eight core chapters follow the supplied Stage 7 Scheme of Work. The Curriculum Framework has 63 Stage 7 subject objectives across the three strands, plus eight Thinking and Working Mathematically practices. The supplied Long-term Planning Template guided the order. The Teacher Guide informed the use of trials, concrete models and prompts that reveal misunderstandings. The teaching and feedback use short British English for readers aged 11–12. [Cambridge programme](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-lower-secondary/curriculum/mathematics/)

The supplied Stage 7 Learner’s Book informed the teaching sequence and representations. The printed contents and sampled pages cover integers and factor tests, equations, rounding and decimals, angle sums and constructions, sampling, fractions, shapes, sequences, percentages, graphs, ratio, chance, transformations, volume and the interpretation of data. The course uses short worked examples followed by a separate opportunity to try the method. [Cambridge resource list](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-lower-secondary/resource-list/cambridge-lower-secondary-mathematics-0862/)

Oak’s explanations informed the use of place-value columns, arithmetic checks, bar models and plotting from a rule. Corbettmaths supplied worked-routine references for negative numbers, factors, fractions, equations, angle facts, transformations and statistics. [Oak Year 7 sequence](https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/programmes/maths-secondary/units?keystages=ks3&years=7) · [Oak arithmetic](https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/programmes/maths-secondary-ks3/units/arithmetic-procedures-with-integers-and-decimals/lessons) · [Oak bar models](https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/programmes/maths-secondary-ks3/units/understanding-multiplicative-relationships-fractions-and-ratio/lessons/determining-the-whole) · [Oak plotting](https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/programmes/maths-secondary-ks3/units/plotting-coordinates/lessons/plotting-a-relationship) · [Corbettmaths topic index](https://corbettmaths.com/contents/)

The written tasks draw on the skills and formats in the supplied 2025 Stage 7 Progression Papers 1 and 2 and their mark schemes from D:/math 7-8. The papers use 50 marks over one hour, with a non-calculator paper and a calculator paper. The older local paper covers and the 2026 paper metadata corroborate the format. Arena questions use independently written contexts, data, diagrams, solutions and indicative marks. Cambridge Progression Tests cover Stages 7–9 and Checkpoint is designed for the end of Stage 9. [Progression Tests](https://help.cambridgeinternational.org/hc/en-gb/articles/360000048158-What-are-the-Cambridge-Lower-Secondary-Progression-Tests) · [Checkpoint age guidance](https://help.cambridgeinternational.org/hc/en-gb/articles/360000055018-Which-age-group-of-learners-is-the-Cambridge-Lower-Secondary-Checkpoint-for)
