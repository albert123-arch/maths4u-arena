# Cambridge IGCSE 0580 · Indices II

Indices II applies the numerical laws of C/E1.7 to algebraic expressions and equations. C2.4 covers integer indices, algebraic products and powers, and direct equations in an index. E2.4 adds algebraic fractional indices and equations requiring a common-base rewrite. The corresponding pages have the same scope in the current and subsequent cycles. [2025–2027 syllabus, pp. 17 and 38](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf) · [2028–2030 syllabus, pp. 17 and 38](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf)

The three Core lessons are followed by two lessons titled Extended. Links to the existing Indices I chapter provide the numerical recap. The new exercises assess algebraic operations or an unknown index. Fractional algebraic powers use positive letter bases. Equations use a common base greater than one, keeping logarithms outside the chapter.

## Exam skill review

The local 0580 Extended snapshot files **01 Number.json** and **02 Algebra and graphs.json** were screened. The Number export groups algebraic index questions with numerical indices, surds and standard form. The following recent items identify the chapter's assessed skills.

- November 2025 Paper 43 Q6 assesses separating the arithmetic coefficient and the matching letter powers in products and quotients.
- June 2025 Paper 42 Q21 assesses a power of a coefficient and an algebraic factor under a fractional exponent.
- November 2025 Paper 23 Q9 assesses the law for quotients when an index is unknown.
- November 2025 Paper 21 Q19 assesses reciprocal and positive common bases. March 2025 Paper 22 Q23 assesses a product before comparing the indices of both sides.
- June 2025 Paper 41 Q21 provides a further example of aligning two different displayed bases. The authored chapter interprets this as common-base work within the syllabus, with a single unknown in the final linear equation.

Archive question text, artwork and marking schemes are absent from the package. Prompts, data, examples, solutions, diagrams and indicative marking criteria were independently prepared.

## Package and validation

Five lessons contain 15 sections, 30 worked examples, 30 in-lesson checks, 30 Extra practice checks, five SVG diagrams and five A4 revision sheets. The task bank provides 25 short questions and their 25 fresh variants, plus 25 written starts with 75 variants. A written task carries 4 or 5 marks across two parts.

For coefficient-form exercises, the exact fields ask for the individual coefficient and exponents. They accept equivalent scalar values, including an exact fraction in a different notation. Full simplified expressions, the requested exponent conventions and method are assessed in the written questions.

Independently parsed symbolic calculations checked 900 generated cases. Tests also covered 174 accepted and 174 rejected field responses, 25 fresh-review routes, 100 written totals and 1,454 rendered fragments. Chromium revision previews fit the 390 px viewport. Each print proof occupies one A4 page. The package is draft source.
