# Cambridge IGCSE 0580 · Money

The chapter covers shared Core and Extended currency calculations and exchange under C/E1.16 and simple and compound interest under C/E1.13. The interest examples use a fixed annual rate. Partial years are used only when the question states that simple interest is proportional to the time. The Percentages chapter covers general reverse percentages. Exponential growth, depreciation and broader repeated-change models belong to E1.17.
