# 0580 source audit for Standard form

## Scope

Cambridge 0580 C1.8 and E1.8 require standard notation, conversion in both directions and calculation. Core calculation belongs to Paper 3. Both specification cycles keep this distinction. The conversion lessons give non-calculator practice, and the calculation lessons teach both exact working and calculator checks. The scale table explains the no-shift identity \(10^0=1\). Assessed standard-form answers use the positive or negative exponents specified for this topic. [Cambridge 2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf), [Cambridge 2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf).

The local Cambridge 0580/0980 Scheme of Work for examination from 2025, p. 44, introduces conversion first, relates it to place value and calculator notation, then develops the four operations. Its discussion of a coefficient below 1 after subtraction informed the separate normalisation step in the final lesson.

## Exam skill audit

The local read-only **01 Number.json** export has 204 questions in **Indices, surds and standard form**. Of the 92 dated 2020–2026, 19 directly mention standard form in the question text. The inspected questions included conversions and counts of placeholder zeros (0580_s21_p22_q10), coefficient correction (0580_w25_p43_q11), combining large powers without expansion (0580_s24_p23_q17 and 0580_m26_p22_q12), and a practical quotient (0580_s25_p43_q6). These identified outcomes and the level of working to assess. The new examples, tasks, figures and criteria use independently created data and wording.

## Package

Four lessons include 24 worked examples, 24 Your turn checks, 24 Extra practice checks and four A4 sheets. Four SVG diagrams support the methods. Maths is rendered in KaTeX and tables use HTML.

The practice manifest has 20 starter questions with 20 fresh variants, plus 80 written questions in 20 families. The written tasks include 160 separately marked parts and 320 indicative Maths4U marks. For standard-form responses the two online fields check the coefficient and exponent independently. Ordinary-number fields compare exact numerical values.

The mathematical check recomputed 272 distinct prompts from their source expressions, checked normalised coefficients, reconciled marks and validated the SVG references. Static checks used the Arena package schema, exact checker and KaTeX renderer. This package awaits registration and an app-preview check.
