# Cambridge IGCSE 0580 · Using a calculator

The shared Core and Extended objective C/E1.14 covers efficient calculator use, appropriate entry and interpretation of the display. The chapter uses full precision until the final answer and distinguishes decimal money from decimal hours. The lessons teach practical input and checking. Methods for arithmetic, estimation and standard form remain in their own Number chapters. For 2025–2027, C/E1.14 is assessed on the calculator paper for each tier. ## Package and validation

`0580-number-calculator` contains four lessons, 12 sections, 24 worked examples, 24 Your turn checks, 24 Extra practice checks and four A4 revision views. The bank has 20 short starts and 20 fresh review variants. It also has 20 written starts with three variants per start and worked answers, hints and marks per part. The four SVGs show input or checking workflows. Formulas use KaTeX.

An independent calculation reproduced 208 written-part results and 88 formative or short-practice results. Runtime checks passed all 88 expected answers and 29 equivalent fraction forms. The 87 recognised wrong answers were rejected. Validation covered the 20 short-to-fresh review links, all marks, 1,263 rendered HTML fragments and all four SVGs.

Each standalone revision preview rendered to a single A4 page with the Arena print styles and its SVG loaded. At 390 CSS pixels the four revision layouts fit the viewport. The exported pages were checked for text and diagram clipping.
