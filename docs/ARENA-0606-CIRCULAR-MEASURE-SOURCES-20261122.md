# 0606 Circular measure

The six lessons follow the ideas in chapter 8 of Sue Pemberton's third-edition *Additional Mathematics Coursebook*, sections 8.1–8.3, and the compound-shape applications at the end of that chapter. They begin with angle units, arc length and sector area, then use triangle geometry, sectors and segments in larger problems. The syllabus requires solving arc and sector problems using radians, including in simple compound shapes. The shared-region problems use equal circles and standard triangle methods. The explanation starts by tracing curved and straight boundaries. Worked problems distinguish a single revolution from total rotation, a chord from an arc, a segment from a sector, and a sector's perimeter from its arc length. The exam problems include multi-step derivations, exact forms and original marking guidance.
