# 0580 Coordinate geometry

The final coverage check adds two Extended lessons for E3.2/E3.5 general-form lines and E3.7 perpendicular bisectors. These extend the graph plotting, gradient and equation rearrangement sequences in both local textbooks and the Scheme of Work. The intercept method starts by setting one coordinate to zero. [Save My Exams coordinate geometry](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/maths/cie/25/extended/topics/coordinate-geometry/), [Corbettmaths gradient and straight-line equations](https://corbettmaths.com/contents/).

The chapter covers C3.1–C3.6 and E3.1–E3.7 in the [Cambridge 2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf). It was cross-checked against the [2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf). The supplied Scheme of Work, PDF pp. 25–26 and 50–51 and the same DOCX sections, separates reading gradients from a grid from calculating them directly from coordinates. Midpoint, length and perpendicular lines are explicitly Extended.
