# 0580 source audit for Direct and inverse proportion

## Scope

Cambridge E2.8 is Extended only. The published objective asks students to translate direct and inverse proportion into algebraic terms and use the relationship to find unknown quantities. The named forms are linear, square, square root, cube and cube root, and students need the proportional sign. All ten forms have their own worked and assessed material in this package. The first two lessons establish the algebraic relationship and the role of the constant after the earlier Number course's comparison and ratio methods. [Cambridge 2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf), [Cambridge 2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf).

The local Cambridge 0580/0980 Scheme of Work for examination from 2025, PDF p. 23, sequences the symbol, equation with an unknown constant, supplied pair and final equation. It highlights confusion between the direct ratio and the inverse product. That distinction structures the worked steps. The chapter restricts evaluated quantities to the positive model branches. In selected shifted examples the domain also keeps the transformed input positive and the denominator nonzero.
