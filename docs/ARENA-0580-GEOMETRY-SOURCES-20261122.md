## Content and checks

The final coverage check adds two worked examples, two checks and two diagrams to the initial set below. These cover regular and irregular polygons and the distinction between congruence and similarity. The two net questions with repeated diagrams now use different valid layouts. Accessible question descriptions identify the faces without naming the solid being asked for.

The chapter has 15 lessons and 90 worked examples, 90 Your turn checks, 90 Extra practice checks and 15 A4 revision sheets. The practice bank has 75 short task families with a fresh variant for each. Its 75 written exam-style families each have a main task and three variants, with a solution and marking criteria for every version. Exercises that require a physical drawing use paper criteria and a model diagram. Concept checks for those exercises cover decisions such as compass width, reference line and fold position.

The canonical package validator, KaTeX renderer, checker and repeat selector verified 450 bank items, 180 guided or extra checks and 241 SVGs. The checker accepted 330 expected inputs and rejected 330 deliberate wrong inputs. The repeat selector supplied an unseen variant for each of the 75 short task families. Independent mathematical models checked 972 generated answers, including the angles read from rendered bearings and full-turn diagrams, and checked the scaled ruler-and-compass geometry for 36 SSS constructions. The browser renders all 15 sheets at 390 px without horizontal overflow. A4 proof PDFs are each one page.
