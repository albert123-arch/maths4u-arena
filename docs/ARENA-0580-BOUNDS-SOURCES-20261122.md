# 0580 Limits of accuracy

Four lessons cover C1.10 and E1.10 of the [Cambridge 2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf). The first two teach a single rounded measurement and error intervals. The second two teach Extended calculations with positive quantities and explicitly carry an Extended label. The [next Cambridge syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf) was also checked. The supplied 0580/0980 Scheme of Work, p. 51 in the PDF, recommends beginning with a single measurement and varying its accuracy before bounds of calculations. The DOCX uses the same sequence.
