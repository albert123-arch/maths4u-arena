# 0580 Statistics teaching and source audit

The Arena `0580-statistics` package covers Section 9 of Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580. The first seven lessons are available at Core and support Extended learners. The next five teach the Extended methods for raw quartiles and interquartile range, estimation from grouped data, cumulative frequency and histograms. Box plots appear in older textbooks and are outside the current Section 9 objectives. [Cambridge syllabus 2025–2027, pp. 30–31 and 55–56](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf), [Cambridge syllabus 2028–2030, Section 9](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf)

The local books are Karen Morrison and Nick Hamshaw, *Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics Core and Extended*, 2nd edition, and David Rayner, Ian Bettison and Mathew Taylor, *Complete Mathematics for Cambridge IGCSE Extended*, 5th edition. Checked classifications, examples, methods and charts for sequencing. The Cambridge 0580/0980 Scheme of Work for examination from 2025 supplied the objective-by-objective teaching links in the middle column below. The package uses newly written explanations, datasets, figures and criteria.

The sources establish skill requirements and the new problems use independently written statements, datasets and illustrations.

There are 12 lessons, 72 worked examples, 72 Your turn checks, 74 extra practice checks and twelve one-page A4 sheets. The native bank contains 360 questions, including 60 short practice questions, 60 linked fresh variants and 240 written questions in 60 four-variant families. The paper exercises ask pupils to construct and check charts as well as calculate.

The independent mathematical audit checked 794 values and items, including median positions, significant outliers, grouped midpoints, pie totals, percentile levels, rates and histogram areas. The 240 written questions carry 880 indicative marks. Arena field-level and KaTeX fixture testing covers the saved answer descriptors and written markup. All numeric tables and stem-and-leaf diagrams use HTML. SVG figures contain charts, scales and labels. The 390 px print fixture uses Arena's revision CSS.
