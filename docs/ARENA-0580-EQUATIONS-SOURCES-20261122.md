# 0580 Equations and formulae

The scope is C2.5 and E2.5 in the [Cambridge 2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf), checked against the [2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf). The first four lessons cover Core and Extended, and four later lessons use Extended material. The supplied 0580/0980 Scheme of Work, PDF p. 21 and the matching DOCX, uses writing a rule, recording operations on both sides and checking results in context. It then moves to simultaneous and quadratic problems and changing the subject. The chapter follows that progression.

The read-only Maths4U export groups Equations, simultaneous equations and formulae and Quadratics and completing the square were used to check skills and typical marking demands. The new items use independently built equations and contexts with fresh values, worked reasoning and Maths4U criteria. The interactive checks demand all roots or coordinate pairs where a question asks for the whole solution. One check asks for the expression for the square before taking a root, while its written counterpart asks for the completed rearrangement.

The package contains eight lessons, 50 worked examples, 100 formative checks, 80 short and review items and 160 written tasks including variants. It has eight revision sheets and six visual diagrams.
