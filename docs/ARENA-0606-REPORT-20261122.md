# Arena 0606 Additional Mathematics

The 16 chapters of Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics are available in the local preview. All 107 lessons are in English, with six worked examples, six inline Your turn checks, six further checks and a printable A4 sheet per lesson. Practice has short answer checks, a fresh variant after a mistake and written exam-style tasks with worked solutions and marking guidance. [Local course](http://127.0.0.1:3117/courses/maths4u-0606)

The course follows the supplied Pemberton coursebook and the current Cambridge 2025–2027 syllabus. The source notes in this directory identify the relevant coursebook sections, Save My Exams explanations and supplementary teaching sources, including TLMaths and Corbettmaths. They describe how the archive informed the tasks. [Course map](ARENA-0606-COURSE-MAP-20261122.md) · [Cambridge syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662470-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf)
