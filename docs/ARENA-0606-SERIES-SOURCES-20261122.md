# Cambridge 0606 series

The chapter follows syllabus objectives 12.1–12.5. It covers the binomial theorem for positive integer powers, arithmetic and geometric progressions, finite sums, and convergence and the sum to infinity of a geometric progression. Terms, coefficients and partial sums are treated separately. [Cambridge syllabus 2025–2027, p. 18](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662470-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf)
