# Cambridge IGCSE 0580 · Differentiation

This package covers E2.12, an Extended-only chapter. Learners estimate a gradient by drawing a tangent, differentiate a power or a sum of up to three terms, work with gradients and stationary points, and identify local maxima and minima. Coefficients are rational and exponents are non-negative integers. The lesson and written work use Cambridge’s derivative notation. [Cambridge 0580 syllabus 2025–2027, p. 41](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf), [Cambridge 0580 syllabus 2028–2030, p. 41](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf)
