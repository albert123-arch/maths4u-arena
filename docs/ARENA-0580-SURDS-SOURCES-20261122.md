# 0580 Surds · sources and checks

Surds is Extended-only E1.18. The current 2025–2027 syllabus lists exact surd manipulation and rationalisation. The 2028–2030 syllabus gives the same coverage. ## Exam skill review

The local Extended file **01 Number.json** gives the following skills and formats.

- June 2025 Paper 43 Q3 calls for identifying an irrational square root.
- March 2026 Paper 22 Q15 and March 2025 Paper 22 Q20 use square-factor extraction before collecting terms.
- June 2025 Paper 21 Q15 and November 2025 Paper 21 Q21 examine expansion and a single-root denominator. The latter explicitly asks for coefficients.
- June 2026 Paper 22 Q17 assesses signed expansion and a two-term denominator. June 2026 Paper 23 Q21 also examines the positive square-root convention and rationalising a rational-minus-root denominator.
- June 2026 Paper 21 Q26 assesses collecting after simplification and rationalisation. The authored problems distinguish choosing an operation from carrying it out.

The package has independently authored explanations, conditions, problem data, diagrams, exact solutions and indicative marking criteria. The local archive references above record skills. The archive question text, diagrams and mark schemes are absent from the content package.

## Answer forms and checks

The exact checker treats equivalent expressions as equal. It accepts an unsimplified root for its simplified numeric equivalent and accepts an irrational-denominator fraction for an equivalent rationalised value. For interactive questions that assess form, the supplied expression includes a fixed square-free radicand and the learner enters its coefficient, the rational and radical coefficients, or the coprime numerator coefficients and positive denominator. Written questions ask the learner to show the complete simplified expression and working, with credit for an equivalent valid method.

The package has five lessons, 30 worked examples, 30 in-lesson checks, 30 extra checks, 25 short questions with 25 fresh variants, and 25 written questions with 75 variants. Five A4 revision sheets produce one page each. Four original SVGs illustrate a number line, grouping, distribution and the conjugate product. Formulae are live KaTeX.

Local checks covered 900 independently parsed symbolic cases, exact input equivalence, 172 correct and 172 incorrect field submissions, 25 routes to fresh variants, 100 paper marking totals and 1,477 HTML renders. Chromium proofs check the revision rendering at 390 px and A4. The package is staged as draft source.
