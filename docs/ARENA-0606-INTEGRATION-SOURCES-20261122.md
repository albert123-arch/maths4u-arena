# 0606 Calculus: Integration

## Sources and teaching sequence

- Cambridge 0606 objectives 14.10–14.13 cover the reverse derivative, powers, the reciprocal, specified functions of linear expressions, definite integration and areas. Arena includes line–curve and curve–curve cases, with every change of sign or ordering split correctly. The lessons stay within the programme's antiderivative rules. [Cambridge 2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662470-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf)
- Sue Pemberton's third-edition coursebook in `D:/new_books/addMaths10.pdf`, §§15.1–15.11, gives the order from reversing differentiation to finding regions. Arena retains the short segments on the constant of integration, linear arguments, exponentials, trigonometric integrals, reciprocals, mixed indefinite and definite exercises, and the graphical choice of bounds. All examples, checks and artwork are independently authored.
- Save My Exams demonstrates separating the antiderivative, the limits and the interpretation of the sign. Arena uses separate checks for these decisions and shows when area requires another subtraction or a split. [Introduction to Integration](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/further-maths/cie/additional-maths/25/revision-notes/calculus/integration/introduction-to-integration/), [Definite Integrals](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/further-maths/cie/additional-maths/25/revision-notes/calculus/integration/definite-integrals/), [Finding Areas with Integration](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/further-maths/cie/additional-maths/25/revision-notes/calculus/integration/finding-areas-with-integration/)
- TLMaths calculates the bounds before finding the area and distinguishes the upper curve from the one being subtracted. Arena's area examples follow the teaching progression and use original graphs with simple readings before requiring deduction. [Integration](https://www.tlmaths.com/home/a-level-maths/full-a-level/h-integration), [Areas Between Curves](https://www.tlmaths.com/home/a-level-maths/teaching-order-year-1/148-integration-areas-between-curves)
