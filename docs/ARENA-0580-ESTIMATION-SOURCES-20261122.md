# 0580 Estimation

Package `0580-number-estimation`, version 1.0.0, covers the three C1.9 and E1.9 objectives. Learners round to a stated precision, estimate by replacing inputs with workable values, and choose appropriate accuracy for a final answer. The current and next syllabus cycles use the same objectives. [2025–2027 syllabus, version 3](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf), [2028–2030 syllabus, version 2](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf)

The local 0580/0980 Scheme of Work, PDF p. 20, places familiar decimal-place rounding before significant figures. It highlights the written zero after carrying into the next place. The current official syllabus gives the usual precision for non-exact answers as three significant figures, with one decimal place for angles in degrees. Where the question gives its own instruction, the lesson follows that instruction. In the local 0580 Number export, I reviewed the Rounding, estimation and bounds group. November 2023 paper 22 question 1 tests two rounding instructions for the same number. June 2024 paper 22 question 6 tests significant figures in a small decimal. June 2023 paper 21 question 4 and March 2025 paper 22 question 9 test rounding inputs while retaining grouping and roots. This is a skill and marking-demand reference. The new tasks, visual designs, worked explanations and criteria were written independently. Limits of accuracy and error intervals begin in C1.10 and E1.10.

The package has 24 worked examples with 24 in-lesson checks and 24 extra checks. Practice has 20 first attempts and 20 fresh forms, plus 20 exam-style problems with three variants each. Four SVG diagrams support the explanations and four A4 revision sheets use the same diagrams. Where visible zeroes determine the required precision, a choice field checks the written form. Numeric fields accept the same exact value in equivalent forms.

The package schema, 80 written marking structures, 20 fresh review selections, 88 correct and 88 wrong field values, and 1206 rendered HTML fragments were checked locally. The four A4 render proofs each fit one page. Chapter position is 8 within the 0580 Number sequence.
