# 0606 Coordinate geometry of the circle

The chapter follows the sequence in chapter 7 of Sue Pemberton's supplied third-edition *Additional Mathematics Coursebook*. It develops the equation of the circle from the distance formula, then introduces line intersections, tangents and common chords. The solutions distinguish line intersections from intersections of the circumferences. Tangent gradients use the perpendicular radius, and the lessons cover horizontal and vertical tangents. [Cambridge 0606 syllabus for 2025–2027, section 8](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662470-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf)

The examples use completing the square, substitution and comparing the centre distance to the radius sum and difference. They progress from matching one diagram to one equation to checking both original circle equations. Equal axis scales preserve the circular shape and perpendicular directions.
