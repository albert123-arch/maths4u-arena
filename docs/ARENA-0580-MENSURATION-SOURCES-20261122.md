# 0580 Mensuration source review

The chapter follows C5.1–C5.5 and E5.1–E5.5. Core starts with units, straight edges, circle measures and fractions of a turn. The local Maths4U exam archive has 338 Mensuration records split into units (75), perimeters and circles (113), and volumes and surfaces (150). The sample includes June 2026 Paper 41 questions 3, 11 and 28, Paper 42 question 16 and Paper 43 question 21. The skills mapped into the new material include choosing a conversion power, converting diameter to radius, separating the two heights of a cone, subtracting a region and identifying exposed faces. Conditions, numerical examples and SVGs are independently authored. The written criteria are Maths4U guidance.

The final coverage check adds Core lessons on perimeters of triangles and quadrilaterals and the area of triangular prism surfaces. Extra practice includes squared millimetre and kilometre conversions and cubed millimetre conversions. The related textbook sections are §7.1 and §7.6 in the first book and pp. 114–118 and 128–136 in the scanned edition.
