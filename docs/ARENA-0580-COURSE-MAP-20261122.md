# Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 in Arena

## Syllabus and teaching order

Use Cambridge's [2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf) for the current examination cycle. Check the [2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf) before adapting the course for the later cycle. Follow the paper and calculator rules for the exam year.

The course begins at a Year 10 entry level. Teach a shared Core method first, then add clearly marked Extended methods where the Cambridge objectives diverge. Use the 0580/0980 Scheme of Work (2025 onwards) as the teaching-order reference. The nine syllabus headings organise the catalogue. The Scheme of Work interleaves topics as prerequisites become available.

## First package — Types of number

C1.1 and E1.1 share the classification and factor skills covered here. The package has five lessons, 32 worked examples, 64 checks in the notes and Extra practice, 50 short practice tasks (25 starts and 25 fresh review questions), 104 written tasks (26 starts and 78 variants), six SVG diagrams and five printable revision sheets. The square and cube material introduces C1.3/E1.3. General powers and roots are a separate topic.

The place-value lesson teaches converting in both directions between words and figures, including numbers with a zero group. The online check asks the learner to choose the matching words. Written practice asks them to produce the words and figures independently. [Cambridge syllabus pp. 12 and 32](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf), [Save My Exams Place Value](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/maths/cie/25/core/revision-notes/number/reading-and-ordering-numbers/place-value/), [Corbettmaths Words and Figures](https://corbettmaths.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Words-Figures.pdf).

## Coordinate geometry

[Coordinate geometry](http://127.0.0.1:3117/courses/maths4u-0580/chapters/coordinate-geometry) covers C3.1–C3.6 and E3.1–E3.7 in ten lessons. Start with axes, gradients, line equations and parallel lines. Extended adds gradient from coordinates, midpoint, length, perpendicular lines and bisectors, and general-form lines.

## Geometry

[Geometry](http://127.0.0.1:3117/courses/maths4u-0580/chapters/geometry) covers C/E4 in 15 lessons. The chapter includes straightedge and compass constructions, nets, scale and bearings, similarity, symmetry, polygon angles and the full Extended set of circle theorems. See the [Geometry source review](ARENA-0580-GEOMETRY-SOURCES-20261122.md).

## Mensuration

[Mensuration](http://127.0.0.1:3117/courses/maths4u-0580/chapters/mensuration) covers C/E5.1–5.5 in 13 lessons. It starts with unit conversion and perimeter, then uses labelled drawings and nets to explain arcs, sectors, area, volume and surface area. Two Extended lessons cover general sectors and frustums. See the [Mensuration source review](ARENA-0580-MENSURATION-SOURCES-20261122.md).

## Trigonometry

[Trigonometry](http://127.0.0.1:3117/courses/maths4u-0580/chapters/trigonometry) has ten lessons. The three shared lessons teach Pythagoras, right-angled trigonometry and practical bearings. Seven Extended lessons cover elevations and shortest distance, exact values, graphs, equations, sine and cosine rules, triangle area and trigonometry in 3D. See the [Trigonometry source review](ARENA-0580-TRIGONOMETRY-SOURCES-20261122.md).

## Transformations and vectors

[Transformations and vectors](http://127.0.0.1:3117/courses/maths4u-0580/chapters/transformations-and-vectors) has ten lessons. The shared route covers translation, reflection, rotation and positive enlargement. Extended adds constructions including negative enlargement, transformations in sequence, vector arithmetic, magnitude, routes and proof. See the [Transformations and vectors source review](ARENA-0580-TRANSFORMATIONS-VECTORS-SOURCES-20261122.md).

## Probability

[Probability](http://127.0.0.1:3117/courses/maths4u-0580/chapters/probability) has nine lessons. Shared material includes probability scales, complements, relative frequency, sample spaces, two-set diagrams and trees with replacement. Extended adds three-set regions, trees without replacement and conditional probability. See the [Probability source review](ARENA-0580-PROBABILITY-SOURCES-20261122.md).

## Statistics

[Statistics](http://127.0.0.1:3117/courses/maths4u-0580/chapters/statistics) has 12 lessons, including averages, data comparison, frequency tables, graphs, pie charts, stem-and-leaf diagrams and scatter graphs. The Extended lessons add quartiles, grouped estimates, cumulative frequency and histograms. Tables and stem-and-leaf diagrams are readable as HTML. Graphs and blank axes for paper practice are SVG. See the [Statistics source review](ARENA-0580-STATISTICS-SOURCES-20261122.md).
