# Cambridge 0606 trigonometry

The chapter follows syllabus objectives 10.1–10.6. It covers all six functions, graphs and transformations using both angle units, amplitude and period, the three squared identities, equations on a given domain and proofs. The modulus of a trig graph is included under objective 1.4. Tangent asymptotes are labelled in sketch questions. Graph multipliers are integers or fractions with the denominators specified by Cambridge. [Cambridge syllabus 2025–2027, pp. 12 and 16–17](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662470-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf)
