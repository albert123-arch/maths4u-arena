# 0580 Ordering

Package `0580-number-ordering`, version 1.0.0, introduces C1.5 and E1.5 in three Year 10 lessons. Both tiers use the same ordering objectives. The lessons apply familiar equivalence and unit conversions where a comparison requires them. Recurring-decimal conversion, operations with signed numbers, indices and algebraic inequality solving have their own places in the course.

- For this examination cycle I checked the [official 2025–2027 syllabus, version 3](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf), C1.5 and E1.5. I checked the [2028–2030 syllabus, version 2](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf) for the next cycle.
- Cambridge's local 0580/0980 Scheme of Work, from 2025, PDF p. 20, starts with comparison cards and asks when several signs can be true. It brings fractions, decimals and percentages onto a common scale, then checks understanding with written practice. In the second lesson the same approach is applied to like measurements.

The local read-only 0580 Number export provided a small skill sample. November 2021 paper 22 question 9 asks for a complete ordering across representations. November 2024 paper 21 question 3 separates the comparison from rounding and checks equality. November 2025 paper 43 question 8 tests comparing proportions on one scale. The new examples, data, diagrams, questions, solutions and marking guidance were written independently. They use the established skills of comparing on a common scale, retaining every value, and showing how a close order was decided.

The local package includes 18 worked examples, 18 in-lesson checks and 18 extra checks, 15 short practice tasks with 15 fresh review questions, and 15 written starts with three variations each. The revision sheets reuse the original number lines and give two checks with answers. The three SVGs use consistent scales and contain only labels that belong to their diagrams.
