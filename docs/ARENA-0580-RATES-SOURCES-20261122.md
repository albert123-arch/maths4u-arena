# 0580 Rates

Package `0580-number-rates`, version 1.0.0, covers C1.12 and E1.12 for Year 10 learners. Both tiers include familiar rates, other compound measures and average speed. Questions provide density, pressure and population-density formulae. Learners practise recalling the speed, distance and time relationship. [2025–2027 syllabus, version 3](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf), [2028–2030 syllabus, version 2](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf)

The local 0580/0980 Scheme of Work, PDF p. 28, introduces speed first as a constant rate, then applies per-unit reasoning to exchange rates, density and filling. The lessons use that relationship between quantities and units. Price-per-pack comparisons belong to C1.11 and E1.11 Ratio and proportion. Later courses develop currency applications, travel graphs and acceleration.
