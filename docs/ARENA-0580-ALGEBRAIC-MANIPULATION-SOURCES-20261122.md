# 0580 source audit for Algebraic manipulation

## Scope and tiers

Cambridge 0580 C2.2 and E2.2 share collecting like terms, expanding products and extracting a complete common factor. The four shared lessons include single brackets with more than one variable and double brackets in one variable. Four separately labelled Extended lessons deepen expansion to products with two letters and at least three brackets, then cover grouping, special products, quadratic and cubic factorisation, and completed-square form. The chapter treats expressions as identities. Solving, inequalities and rearranging formulas belong to the later equations module. Algebraic fractions have their own E2.3 chapter. [2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf), [2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf).

The local Cambridge 0580/0980 Scheme of Work for examination from 2025, PDF pp. 10–11, starts with signed coefficients and distribution, introduces the two-by-two grid, and uses factorisation as the inverse of distribution. Its Extended sequence informed the move from longer products to grouping, the two square identities, and the split-middle-term method before completing the square. Current syllabus objectives determine tier placement.
