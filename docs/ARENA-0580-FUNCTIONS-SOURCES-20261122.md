# Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 E2.13 Functions

Six lessons for Extended. Package `0580-functions`, chapter `igcse_0580_functions`, position 30. The study material uses Cambridge notation and the examples, diagrams and practice were written independently. The local exam archive supplied skill-level evidence.

## Scope and teaching sources

The operative syllabus asks learners to understand function, domain, range and function notation, find inverses and form composites. Mapping diagrams are included. An official example uses a rational composite presented as one fraction. The lesson on checking a proposed input illustrates how restrictions survive substitution and simplification. No question requires calculating the whole domain or range of a composite. The package includes no transformations of function graphs or logarithmic inverses. [Cambridge syllabus 2025–2027, E2.13, p. 42](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf), [Cambridge syllabus 2028–2030, E2.13, p. 42](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf)

Read the local Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics Core and Extended textbook, §22.3, printed pp. 546–553, PDF pp. 556–563. The book progresses from function notation and substitution to composite notation, then compares finding the inverse by a flow diagram with rearranging to recover the original input. Read the local Oxford Complete Mathematics for Cambridge IGCSE, Fifth Edition Extended, §8.6, printed pp. 305–310, PDF pp. 315–320. Its function-machine examples connect substitution and the order of operations, and its composite and inverse examples connect the diagrams to algebra.

Read the Cambridge 0580/0980 Scheme of Work for examination from 2025, §10 E2.13, p. 58. It introduces functions for specific values with mapping notation, develops inverse operations and rearrangement, and compares opposite orders of linear or quadratic composition. That sequence informs the following lessons. The local papers and lesson files retain their original locations.
