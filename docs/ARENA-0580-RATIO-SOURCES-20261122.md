# 0580 Ratio and proportion

C1.11 and E1.11 share the same objectives in the [Cambridge 2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf). The four lessons teach ordered ratios, division in a given ratio, recipe adaptation, map scales and best value. The [2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/745681-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf) was also checked. The 0580/0980 Scheme of Work from 2025, p. 27 of the supplied PDF, starts with simplifying ratios and then moves to sharing real amounts and comparing value. The supplied DOCX was checked for the same activities. Rates with compound units and algebraic direct and inverse proportion use their own later objectives.
