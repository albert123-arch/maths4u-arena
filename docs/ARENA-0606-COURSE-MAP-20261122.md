# Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics 0606 in Arena

The course follows the current 2025–2027 Cambridge syllabus and the 16-chapter progression in Sue Pemberton, *Cambridge IGCSE and O Level Additional Mathematics Coursebook*, third edition. The supplied book is D:/new_books/addMaths10.pdf. The course groups simultaneous equations with quadratics and splits the calculus objectives between two differentiation chapters, integration and kinematics. The later syllabus is tracked separately. A short practice check and its fresh variant share a skill and use different inputs. The written tasks have worked methods, final answers and part-level marking guidance.
