# Cambridge IGCSE 0580 · Algebraic fractions

This is E2.3, an Extended-only topic. The current and next syllabus both require manipulation of algebraic fractions and factorisation and simplification of rational expressions. Numerical fractions provide a short bridge to common polynomial denominators. Solving algebraic-fraction equations is covered separately with equations.
